Apollo X is a Shoot ’em up game in which the player tries to conquer other planets of the galaxy. It has 2 modes: Story and Infinite. In the Story mode you unlock planets along with your progress. In the Infinite mode you simply try to survive while gathering materials for reparing your ship and recover all the health (the health you loose in story mode won’t recover at the end of the levels).

This game was made by 4 multimedia students, being the first game we ever made. The game was very simple but it was finished as a product.
